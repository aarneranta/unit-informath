Gauss's lemma and polynomial gcd domains: from Thierry Coquand's checker Unit, through Dedukti, to English by Informath

The development sag/gcddomain of Unit proves Gauss's lemma and that a polynomial ring over a gcd domain is a gcd domain. Thierry Coquand has written its proof informally, file by file: Gauss's lemma and polynomial gcd domains: an informal proof following the Unit files. Here the development is translated to Dedukti and read by Informath, and its proofs are compared with that text.
